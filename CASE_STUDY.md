# Superstore Sales Dashboard – Case Study

## 1. Background

Retail businesses generate a large amount of sales data every day. This data can help understand what products are performing well and where improvements are needed.

This project analyzes Superstore sales data using Excel to find simple but useful business insights.

---

## 2. Problem

Businesses often face questions like:

* Which products generate the most profit?
* Which categories are performing well or poorly?
* How do discounts affect profit?
* Which regions are more profitable?

The goal of this project was to answer these questions using a simple Excel dashboard.

---

## 3. Dataset

The dataset includes:

* Order ID
* Product name
* Category and sub-category
* Sales amount
* Profit
* Discount
* Region
* Order date

Some basic cleaning was done inside Excel before analysis.

---

## 4. Approach

### Data preparation

* Removed duplicates
* Checked missing values
* Standardized data format

### Analysis

Focused on:

* Sales and profit comparison
* Category and sub-category performance
* Regional performance
* Impact of discount on profit

### Dashboard building

Created an Excel dashboard using:

* Pivot tables
* Pivot charts
* Slicers for filtering

---

## 5. Findings

### Profit is not always aligned with sales

Some products have high sales but low profit due to discounts.

---

### Sub-categories drive performance differences

A few sub-categories contribute most of the profit, while others underperform.

---

### Regional differences exist

Some regions consistently generate better profit compared to others.

---

### Discounts reduce profitability

High discount rates often lead to lower profit margins.

---

## 6. Business insights

The analysis shows that focusing only on sales is not enough. Profitability should also be considered when making business decisions.

Discount strategy and product selection play a big role in overall performance.

---

## 7. Recommendations

* Reduce unnecessary discounting on low-margin products
* Focus on high-profit sub-categories
* Monitor regional performance regularly
* Balance sales growth with profit stability

---

## 8. Conclusion

This project shows how Excel can be used to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights. Even simple tools can provide strong decision support when used properly.

---

## 9. What I learned

* How to build dashboards in Excel
* How to use pivot tables for analysis
* How to interpret business performance data
* How to connect data with business decisions
